Vibe Toxicology LIS — presentation credits and source notices

Presentation: the original demo-video/out/vibe-tox-overview.mp4.
The original screen recording uses the real React interface with a mocked engine and synthetic data. The narrated final was assembled by demo-video/mix.mjs from the silent recording, generated voice clips and original instrumental music.

Narration
Generated locally with Kokoro v1.0, voice af_heart, speed 1.0, through kokoro-onnx. This provenance is recorded in demo-video/README.md, audio/narration.json and audio/tts.py. Kokoro-82M is an Apache-2.0 model:
https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M
https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M/blob/main/LICENSE
The narration is synthetic; it is not a recording of a laboratory employee or patient.

Music
Original instrumental composition created in demo-video/audio/make_music.py and rendered with FluidSynth using the MuseScore General soundfont. The source script and harness README identify the composition as original, with no third-party music recording used.

Soundfont
MuseScore General, version 0.2 (13 May 2020). The complete source notice is reproduced below with line endings and trailing whitespace normalized, including the acknowledgements for Frank Wen, Michael Cowgill, S. Christian Collins, Ethan Winer and Michael Schorsch, and the MIT permission and warranty text.
https://musescore.org/en/handbook/3/soundfonts-and-sfz-files
https://ftp.osuosl.org/pub/musescore/soundfont/MuseScore_General/MuseScore_General_License.md

Caption and transcript preparation
The original narration script and production timeline were reused. Phrase boundaries were aligned using the existing local mlx-whisper installation and its cached whisper-small-mlx model. Automated word timings were checked against the source words and clip boundaries; they remain estimates of speech timing.

---

Current version: 0.2  13th May 2020

This is a scaled-down version of **MuseScore_General-HQ.sf2** that replaces some of the larger instruments to save memory and CPU on older PCs. This SoundFont is currently a work-in-progress.

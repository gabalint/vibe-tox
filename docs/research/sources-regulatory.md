Agency guidance explains a rule but does not replace controlling law. eCFR describes itself as authoritative but unofficial; use controlling published texts and amendments for legal review.

Where no publication/effective date was established, the date column explicitly says so. Checked date means consulted during this research, not that the source's text was published that day. Search-index excerpts were used only where indicated. No private or licensed checklist material was obtained or copied.

## Refresh protocol

Before a production release or a new state, program or laboratory type, reopen the controlling sources and record: checked date, issuing authority, publication date, effective date, compliance date, affected text, status, applicable entities, and impacted REG IDs. Keep proposals, final rules, vacated rules, guidance and accredited-program requirements separately labeled. Escalate conflicts to the accountable human owner; an agent must not resolve legal conflicts by silently selecting the easiest rule.

Priority rechecks: FDA LDT/software status; current HIPAA rulemaking; CMS-3478-P; Part 2 accounting implementation; DOT forms/panels/laboratory and device availability; state requirements; target CAP checklist/ISO edition; payer/MAC policies. Further rechecks: MAC LCD/article versions for the target jurisdictions; HTI-5/HTI-6; the 2021 Privacy Rule final and the timely-access NPRM; the Section 504 final rule on compliance dates; CIRCIA; PAMA's next reporting period and SALSA; state reportable-result lists for the selected menu; all source-summary and snippet rows that decide a design question.

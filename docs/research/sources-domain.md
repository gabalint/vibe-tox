## Evidence gaps to close with a design-partner laboratory

- Signed test menu, matrices, method versions, instrument/software inventory, example export files, maintenance and calibration procedures.
- Real workflow observation: volume, batch sizes, reviewers, exception rates, referral arrangements, turnaround commitments, label media and scanners.
- Approved result terminology, reporting limits, significant digits, dilution rules, cutoff boundary behavior, reflex authority, report examples and corrected-report policy.
- EHR/billing counterpart implementation guides, synthetic messages, acknowledgement rules and acceptance test contacts.
- Applicable accreditation checklists and licensed standards. Their full contents have not been assumed from public summaries.
- Drug-monitoring policy inputs: target payer/MAC set and order-completeness rules, the licensed or payer-specified drug-class source and analyte mapping, date-of-service rule, medication terminology and alias list, medication-consistency rule content and vocabulary (if offered), validity categories and report consequences per matrix, menu-change governance and retained-specimen policy.
- Current emerging-substance surveillance (for example CFSRE/NPS Discovery trend reports, whose source returned HTTP 403 at the recorded check date) and any medetomidine evidence; not verified here.

These gaps prevent final assay configuration, partner interface contracts and operational sign-off. They do not prevent the proposed architecture and requirements from being reviewed.

## Order and accession flow recheck, 2026-10-14

Existing DOM-010/DOM-011 were rechecked on 2026-10-14 against HL7 FHIR R4 4.0.1 (published 2019-11-01). [ServiceRequest](https://hl7.org/fhir/R4/servicerequest.html) describes requested service; [Specimen definitions](https://hl7.org/fhir/R4/specimen-definitions.html) distinguishes the laboratory's accession identifier from the specimen identifier and collection time from receipt time. ServiceRequest can reference multiple specimens, and Specimen can reference multiple requests: a universal one-order/one-accession/one-container relationship is not established. These are exchange semantics, not a mandated internal schema or laboratory grouping policy.

Existing [SR-003 and SR-004](sources-regulatory.md) were also rechecked on 2026-10-14: [42 CFR 493.1241](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-493.1241) requires authorized requests and accurate transcription; [42 CFR 493.1242](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-493.1242) requires applicable written specimen-handling/acceptance policies and documented receipt date/time. US federal CLIA context; retrieved eCFR text was current through 2026-10-08 and 2026-10-09 respectively, not new section effective dates. Neither source prescribes separate screens; state and laboratory-policy applicability remains unresolved.

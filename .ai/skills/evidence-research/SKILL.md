# Evidence research

Read [research conventions](../../../docs/research/README.md) and the relevant source ledger. State the decision the research should inform and its jurisdiction/program/version assumptions.

- Use primary regulator, standards-body, vendor or maintainer documents for consequential claims. Record a direct URL, checked date, publisher, supported claim and limits; capture effective dates separately from publication/crawl dates.
- Follow decisive search results to their source. If inaccessible, identify what was and was not verified. Do not infer absence of a product feature from silence on its website.
- Compare alternatives against the same workflow questions. Label vendor advertising and distinguish it from tested capability.
- Preserve conflicting sources and date/version differences. Explain what evidence would resolve the conflict.
- Update the canonical synthesis and source ledger together. Put unresolved decisions in [open questions](../../../docs/planning/open-questions.md).

Return findings, sources, confidence limits and decision implications. Keep provider terms, rule status and dependency support date-bound. Never claim exhaustive regulatory or technical coverage. Research does not authorize vendor outreach, purchases or publication.
